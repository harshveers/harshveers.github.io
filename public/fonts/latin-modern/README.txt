Latin Modern Roman 10 (Regular, Bold, Italic, Bold Italic), version 2.004, by B. Jackowski and
J. M. Nowacki for GUST, the Polish TeX Users Group. This is the typeface of the LaTeX resume.

These files are derived from lmroman10-*.otf in TeX Live 2025: subset to Latin characters and
converted to WOFF2 (fontTools pyftsubset). The glyph designs are unchanged. As the license requests
for derived works, they carry new file names (lmr-web-*.woff2).
